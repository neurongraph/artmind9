Entity Classes 18

CASE

A formally opened, named incident, complaint, or investigation record — identified by a case ID (e.g., CASE-2026-041) — that ties together the incident timeline, customer complaint, impact assessment, remediation, and retention decision under one owner and status. Extract the case itself as a top-level entity, distinct from any single event, complaint, or decision within it.

data_exposure_casesecurity_incident_casefraud_casecustomer_complaint_caseregulatory_investigation_case
Properties (7)
  • case_id — e.g., CASE-2026-041
  • status — open | under_investigation | active_hold | closed
  • opened_date
  • owner — the role accountable for the case, e.g., Incident Commander
  • workstream_owners — list of teams/roles and what each owns, e.g., "CTO: containment", "DPO: privacy and holds"
  • related_documents — linked overview, timeline, complaint, or retention documents
  • status_note — any caveat on the status, e.g., "forensic reconciliation pending"

INCIDENT_EVENT

A discrete, timestamped step in an incident's timeline — a detection, severity classification, containment action, forensic referral, reconciliation finding, or escalation — as recorded in an incident timeline or log. Extract each timeline row or log entry as its own entity, preserving its date/time.

  • actor_or_team — who performed or observed it
  • outcome — what resulted from this step

CUSTOMER_COMPLAINT

A customer's expression of dissatisfaction or a formal request — for an impact explanation, compensation, subject access, or erasure — raised in connection with a case. Extract as an entity distinct from the case itself, capturing what the customer is asking for and how it is being handled.

IMPACT_ASSESSMENT

A stated finding of how many customers, accounts, or records were affected by an incident, attributed to the team that produced it (e.g., Security's initial count vs. Operations' reconciled count). Extract each distinct assessment as its own entity rather than collapsing conflicting counts into one final number — the conflict and its pending reconciliation are themselves facts to preserve.

  • basis — what the count measures, e.g., "potentially affected" vs. "confirmed affected"
  • reconciliation_status — pending | reconciled
  • excluded_or_adjusted — anything removed or adjusted from a prior count, and why

REMEDIATION_ACTION

Abstract parent domain for FirstUK Bank's banking corpus.
