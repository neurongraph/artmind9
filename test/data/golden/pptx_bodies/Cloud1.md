# Hybrid Cloud Migration Program: Large UK Bank

Business Challenge

- A history of mergers and acquisitions across the group had led to an IT estate that is significantly larger and more complex than is optimal for the bank.
- Increased change and run cost to maintain the estate
- Increased risk due to diluted SME knowledge &amp; aging technology
- Large amount of technical debt causing support and regulatory issues

[picture]

IBM Differentiator

- Migrate applications from traditionally managed infrastructure to an IBM provisioned hybrid cloud
- Developing 15+ technical patterns that helps the bank to lead the industry (thru agility, flexibility, reliability and faster time to market)
- Move/Upgrade applications from out of support H/W &amp; S/W currency to reduce technical debt
- Unique methods and tools for migration; Factory capability to deliver migrations at pace
- Breadth of consultancy and transformational capability

Complexity

25K Hosts &amp; ~1300 Apps to be migrated are classified into

1. Re-Hosting (like-to-like) - 1500 hosts; 100 Apps
2. Re-Factoring (W2K, W2K3 or W2K8 to W2012 etc.) – 21000 hosts; 1000 apps
3. Re-Platform (AIX to Linux) – All Unix to Linux platform – 3500 hosts; 200 Apps

Outcome

Implement Target Operating Model to enable bank to maximize investment in Cloud across all their lines of business.

Work with existing application run and change teams to deliver application remediation where required to move applications to the target within the hybrid cloud

Business Benefits

Technical debt reduction resolving a large platform currency issue within the bank

Reduction in infrastructure RTB cost

Faster environment provision thus reducing CTB cost

Allow the bank to capitalize on their investment in Cloud by tuning their processes across all lines of business

Engagement

Multi year strategic partnership

Global (Managed) Delivery

One IBM (GBS/GTS)

Large pool of highly skilled architects &amp; consultants are involved in this complex program

- Prepare &amp; Execute
- Defect Management

Handover application     to BAU	

Activities:

- Runbook
- Prepare &amp; Execute &amp; Cutover Handover To BAU, Clean

Decommission

Activities:

- Decommission from the old server
- CLEAN the infra/servers

Within the 26 weeks from Order Submission (Change Factory) to Wave Complete there is a Chill period of 12 weeks in which change should be minimized as much as possible. This is followed by the Freeze period of 14 weeks during which no changes are permitted in the source environment, followed by 2 weeks of after care during which production promotion is limited to defect remediation in the Cloud.
