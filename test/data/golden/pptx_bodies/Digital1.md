# Lloyds Banking Group

The Digital IVR

- In 2017, we were tasked with reducing call volume into Telephony for Retail Banking at Lloyds Banking Group.
- The outcome of this exploration was the ideation of a Digital IVR (DIVR) promoting self serve for all customers at the first instance, before handing across to a live agent when suitable, essentially an Ask me Anything Virtual Assistant.
- By placing messaging as the central entry point for all conversations with the bank, we ensure their customers are able to engage with their bank in their preferred manner and, as the service is available 24/7, they can interact when it suits them.
- During the engagement, IBM delivered Industry leading Conversation Architecture to improve accuracy of NLU and built in exception handling to ensure the DIVR never deals with queries better suited for a human.
- Starting with Bank of Scotland for Android customers, we scaled across all 3 core brands (Lloyds, Halifax and Bank of Scotland) and expanded to users for both Android and iOS.
